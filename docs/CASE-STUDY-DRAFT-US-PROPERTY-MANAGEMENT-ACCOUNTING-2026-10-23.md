## Short summary
Accounstone supports recurring accounting operations for a California real estate investment company that has its own property management company and manages residential and commercial property activity. The documented workflow includes transaction review and posting in Yardi, daily bank reconciliations, tenant-ledger updates, payroll journal-entry preparation from ADP exports, month-end close, owner financial reporting, lender-support schedules and year-end coordination with the client's external CPA.

This case study describes the work performed and the controls around it. It does not claim a measured financial or operational outcome.

## The operating challenge
Property accounting requires transactions from several sources to agree: bank activity, deposit records, tenant ledgers, payroll exports, loan statements, property-level records and the general ledger. A posting error or missing source document can affect tenant balances, cash visibility, owner reporting or period-end review.

The engagement spans both property-level activity and the operating company's own accounting, so the workflow must keep property records, management-company expenses and intercompany allocations distinct. The recurring workload also spans several cadences. Rent and receipts are processed on specific dates; bank activity is reconciled daily; payroll-related entries and reimbursements recur biweekly; month-end requires formal reconciliations and financial reports; and quarterly and annual cycles involve lender support, year-end tie-outs and the CPA handoff.

## Accounstone's documented scope

### Daily transaction processing and controls
- Prepare journal entries for intercompany transfers using the client's transaction reports.
- Review deposit batches and supporting copies for residential rent payments made by check or money order before posting in Yardi.
- Flag discrepancies to the property manager for clarification rather than silently posting questionable items.
- Post commercial tenant ACH receipts and maintain the related tenant accounting records.
- Record returned tenant payments using the designated NSF workflow, notify the property manager and update tenant ledger balances.
- Reconcile bank activity to check registers daily to keep cash records current.
- Prepare accounting records for inter-account transfers in line with the client's established instructions and authorization controls.

### Payroll and management-company allocations
- Review a CSV export from ADP and prepare the management company's payroll journal entry for posting in Yardi.
- Compile property-to-management-company labor reimbursement support for accounts payable.
- Prepare labor-cost allocation worksheets for construction-related work using information supplied by HR.

### Monthly close and owner reporting
- Complete the formal month-end bank reconciliation process after daily reconciliation activity.
- Post recurring property-tax and insurance journal entries, loan service charges, rent batches and late-charge batches according to the client's calendar.
- Review general-ledger postings, investigate or document discrepancies, and support month-end close.
- Prepare owner-facing income statements, balance sheets and cash-flow reports.
- Prepare management-fee worksheets and provide the supporting details for the client's accounts-payable process.
- Prepare sales-tax filing support and communicate payment and liability-entry details to the client.
- Record payments received from a third-party collection agency.

### Quarterly and annual support
- Assemble lender reserve reimbursement support using invoices and cancelled-check evidence.
- Tie lender principal and interest records to the books and prepare correcting journal entries when supported by the records and authorized for posting.
- Prepare bad-debt write-off entries for client review and authorization.
- Prepare job-level close and profit/loss records for completed construction jobs from the supplied records.
- Review the general ledger, tie the trial balance to the financial statements and organize the year-end package for the external CPA.
- Record CPA-provided year-end adjustments as authorized and route tax-liability instructions to the client's accounts-payable process.

## How the working controls fit together
The process uses source-document review, scheduled posting, reconciliation, exception communication and review-ready reporting. Where a deposit record does not agree with the batch, the discrepancy is sent to the property manager. Bank reconciliation supports cash visibility, while the month-end general-ledger review and trial-balance tie-out support the reporting package. Lender records and CPA-provided adjustments are reconciled to the books and handled through the client's review and authorization process.

The client and its authorized personnel retain responsibility for approving payments and transfers, controlling bank access, deciding tax positions, approving write-offs and journal entries, and making final accounting or professional judgments. The external CPA retains responsibility for their own professional work and sign-off.
