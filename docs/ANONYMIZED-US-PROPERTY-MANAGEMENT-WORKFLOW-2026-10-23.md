## Workflow map

### Daily operations
- Prepare journal entries for intercompany transfers from transaction reports.
- Review and post residential rent deposit batches in Yardi using copies of checks/money orders and deposit records supplied by property management; communicate discrepancies to the manager before posting.
- Post tenant-originated ACH receipts for commercial properties.
- Record returned/NSF tenant payments in Yardi using the designated NSF process, notify the property manager and update tenant ledger balances.
- Reconcile bank activity to check registers on a daily cadence.
- Record inter-account cash transfers according to the client's documented threshold, schedule and authorization controls. The property manager/authorized client personnel retain approval and transfer authority.

### Biweekly operations
- Prepare the management company's payroll journal entry from an ADP-exported CSV, checking the file before posting to Yardi.
- Compile property-to-management-company labor reimbursements from payroll data for the accounts-payable process.
- Prepare supporting labor-cost reimbursement worksheets for construction-related costs allocated to properties, using information provided by HR.

### Monthly close and reporting
- Post the formal month-end bank reconciliations after ongoing daily reconciliation work.
- Post recurring property-tax and insurance journal entries on the client's schedule.
- Record loan-statement service charges.
- Prepare and post late-charge batches and residential/commercial rent batches according to the client's calendar.
- Review the general ledger for posting accuracy and complete month-end close.
- Produce owner-facing income statements, balance sheets and cash-flow reports.
- Prepare management-fee calculations/worksheets for the accounts-payable process after bank reconciliations are complete.
- Prepare sales-tax return support for a property/client-specific filing, coordinate payment/reporting, and provide the paid amount and supporting report so the client records the related liability entry.
- Record receipts from a third-party collections agency.

### Quarterly reporting and lender support
- Produce owner financial reports.
- Assemble lender reserve-account reimbursement packages, including invoices and cancelled-check evidence, for the client's review/submission process.

### Annual close and CPA coordination
- Tie lender principal and interest records to the books and prepare correcting journal entries as needed.
- Review intercompany loan balances and liabilities for settlement.
- Prepare tenant bad-debt write-off entries for authorized client review.
- Close completed construction jobs and prepare job-level profit/loss recognition entries based on approved records.
- Review the general ledger, tie the trial balance to the income statement and balance sheet, and prepare the year-end accounting package for the external CPA.
- Coordinate CPA questions with owner involvement, post CPA-provided entries as authorized, and give accounts payable the tax-liability information supplied by the CPA.
