# Competitor SEO Review: Industry Pages

**Reviewed:** 9 October 2026  
**Scope:** Qualitative review of publicly accessible pages surfaced in search. This is not a verified rank-position report; results vary by query, location and device.

## Competitor pages reviewed

- [REA — Outsourced Real Estate Accounting](https://rea.co/): lists monthly-close tasks, AP/AR, reconciliations, accruals, owner draws, software platforms, process steps and FAQs.
- [REA — RealPage accounting](https://rea.co/realpage/): connects a specific property-management platform to accounting workflow and data structure.
- [Gallagher & Mohan — Real Estate Accounting Division](https://www.gallaghermohan.com/kpo-divisions/real-estate-accounting-division/): describes offshore real estate accounting for owners, operators, developers, fund managers and property managers.
- [Dynamite Management — HOA accounting providers](https://dynamitemanagement.com/blog/best-hoa-accounting-service-providers-2026/): compares service scope, reporting, AP/AR, reconciliations, reserve/operating fund tracking, controls and pricing. It is published by a provider that ranks itself first, so its rankings and comparative claims should be treated as self-interested.
- [Neowork — Real Estate Accounting Outsourcing Companies in India](https://www.neowork.com/insights/real-estate-accounting-outsourcing-companies-india): discusses property/entity books, CAM, lease-related work, reconciliations and portfolio reporting.

## Findings and recommended actions

### HOA accounting

Competitor pages make recurring deliverables and control boundaries concrete: monthly board reports, assessment balances, AP/AR, bank reconciliations, separate reserve and operating activity, and audit-support schedules.

Accounstone already covers most of these topics. Keep strengthening links from the HOA industry page to the month-end checklist, homeowner ledgers, receivables aging, bank reconciliation, reserve accounting and board-reporting guides. Keep board approvals and payment release clearly separated from preparation work.

Do not copy competitor pricing, credentials, fraud-reduction percentages, tax-filing promises or owner-portal claims without evidence that Accounstone can support them.

### Real estate accounting

Competitor pages focus on property-level and entity-level accounting rather than generic bookkeeping. Useful details include mortgage and escrow records, closing-statement detail, property-level reconciliations, AP/AR, investor/lender reporting, month-end close and year-end schedules for the client's CPA.

Accounstone's real-estate page already separates owners, investors and developers from the HOA and property-management audiences. Keep that distinction and link to the relevant service and software pages. Do not use unverified staff counts, property counts, client outcomes or cost-savings figures.

### Property management and software-specific pages

Strong software pages explain the actual workflow around the named platform; they do not simply repeat the product name. Accounstone's Yardi/Texas page should explain the tasks supported, the intended reader, and which details are confirmed during scoping. Avoid implying a Texas office or state-specific tax service unless established.

Keep platform claims limited to products and workflows the team can support. Link software-specific content to the main property-management and real-estate pages.

### CPA-firm outsourcing

Useful competitor patterns include clear handoff steps, software familiarity, defined preparation tasks and a review process. Accounstone should continue explaining how the firm's checklists, accepted sample files, review notes and open-item escalation guide the handoff. Avoid guarantees about turnaround or error-free work.

## Priorities

1. **Completed:** revised the HOA reserve-adequacy FAQ in neutral language. It explains that reserve adequacy is assessed through a qualified reserve study, funding is a board decision, and the accounting role is to maintain clear fund records.
2. Keep the existing HOA controls and board-package content; it already addresses many topics seen on competitor pages.
3. Maintain separate intent for HOA accounting, property management and real estate accounting.
4. Build software-specific pages only when they describe a real workflow and provide distinct, supportable information.
5. Publish pricing or case studies only when the figures are approved and assumptions are clear.
6. After the Vercel build limit clears, verify the production build, canonical URLs, redirects, schema and rendered content before judging performance or ranking changes.

## Measurement limits

This review does not establish that a competitor ranks above Accounstone for any specific keyword, and it does not estimate search volume. It is based on public pages surfaced in the current search. Actual comparisons require repeated query/location-specific SERP checks or a rank-tracking setup.
