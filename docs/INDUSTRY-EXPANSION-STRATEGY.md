# Industry Expansion Strategy — U.S. Accounting Outsourcing

Updated: 2026-10-12

## Purpose

Expand industry coverage based on distinct buyer intent and demonstrable accounting workflow needs, not a fixed page quota. The immediate target market is the United States. Keep SEO (query intent, page ownership, internal links, metadata and indexability) separate from AEO (clear direct answers, definitions, evidence and structured information).

## What competitor research does and does not establish

The October 12 search review surfaced dedicated industry/service pages for construction accounting, e-commerce accounting and property/real-estate accounting. Search-result appearance is evidence that competitors actively publish around these topics; it is not a verified organic rank position, search volume, traffic estimate or proof that Accounstone can rank.

- REA real estate accounting: https://rea.co/ and https://rea.co/accounting/ — property-level close, AP/AR, reconciliations, owner distributions and software-specific workflows.
- Analytix real estate/property management: https://www.analytix.com/industry/real-estate — CAM reconciliation, month-end close, owner financials and named property platforms.
- QX Global Group real estate accounting: https://qxglobalgroup.com/usa/industry/real-estate-accounting-services — separates property segments such as multifamily, student housing, senior living, HOAs and commercial property.
- BPM construction outsourcing: https://www.bpm.com/services/accounting/outsourced-accounting/construction/ — job-cost analysis, WIP schedules, AP/AR, payroll processing and month-end close.
- Vance & Cole construction accounting: https://vancecole.com/industries/construction-accounting — job costing, WIP schedules, progress billing and retainage.
- Acgile e-commerce accounting: https://www.acgile.com/services/ecommerce-accounting — marketplace settlements, SKU-level COGS, channel reporting and payment/platform reconciliation.
- OM Accounting e-commerce for U.S. businesses: https://omaccounting.in/usa/ — demonstrates direct competition from India-based providers around marketplace settlement reconciliation, inventory/COGS and tax-ready books.

These pages inform content structure and topic relevance only. Competitor performance metrics, claims, credentials and pricing must not be copied or repeated as Accounstone facts.

## Current industry architecture

Existing primary commercial pages:
- `/industries/cpa-firms` — accounting practices and firms as the buyer.
- `/industries/technology` — technology and SaaS businesses.
- `/industries/healthcare` — medical practices and healthcare providers.
- `/industries/ecommerce` — online retailers and marketplace sellers.
- `/industries/real-estate` — owners, investors, developers and commercial operators.
- `/industries/property-management` — firms managing property for owners.
- `/industries/hoa-accounting` — associations and their managers.
- `/industries/professional-services` — law firms, consultants and agencies.

Preserve the audience split between real-estate owners, third-party property managers and HOAs. Do not fold those audiences back into one broad page.

## Priority roadmap

### Tier 1 — Build and validate
1. **Construction & contractors — `/industries/construction` (first new page).**
   - Commercial intent: outsourced construction accounting/bookkeeping support.
   - Distinct mechanics: job/project-level cost coding, subcontractor/vendor bills, retainage tracking, progress-billing records, payroll-cost allocation, bank reconciliation and period-end project reporting.
   - WIP schedules and revenue recognition must be described as records/schedule support using the client's approved accounting policy and CPA/management review—not independent accounting-policy, tax, bonding or surety advice.
   - Mention Buildertrend or JobTread only as client-side project systems whose reports/exports may feed the workflow; do not claim native integrations unless verified.
2. **Strengthen existing e-commerce page before adding subpages.**
   - Compare its settlement, returns/refunds, gateway fees, inventory/COGS and channel-level reporting coverage with the competitor pages above.
   - Do not add separate Amazon, Shopify or Walmart service pages until evidence shows distinct commercial intent and enough original workflow depth.
3. **Strengthen existing healthcare page with clear boundaries.**
   - Payer/claims data can be sensitive. Do not imply HIPAA compliance or PHI handling unless the engagement's controls and contractual posture are verified. Focus on finance records, payer/patient receivables, adjustments and reporting.

### Tier 2 — Research before approving URLs
- **Home services and specialty trades:** research ServiceTitan/Housecall Pro workflows, technician/job costing, invoice-to-payment reconciliation, merchant fees and payroll allocation. Keep separate from construction only if the buyer and accounting workflows differ enough to warrant a distinct service page.
- **Nonprofit accounting:** investigate fund accounting, restricted/unrestricted activity, grant schedules, donor restrictions and Form 990 support. Create only after confirming delivery capability and defining the boundary between bookkeeping schedules and tax filing/advice.
- **Manufacturing/distribution:** investigate inventory costing, landed costs, COGS, purchase/receipt matching and multi-warehouse records. Do not launch until relevant experience and scope are confirmed.
- **Hospitality/senior living:** competitor pages exist in the real-estate accounting category, but this is not enough to justify a new Accounstone page without evidence of service fit.

### Local pages
Continue the existing state → county/metro → city research process for HOA and property management. San Diego County HOA accounting remains a research candidate, not an automatic next page. Compare the California state page, competitor geography and local accounting context before approval. Never imply a local office, staff or on-site service.

## Page-quality requirements

Every new industry page must:
1. Own a distinct commercial intent and not duplicate a current industry/service page.
2. Explain the actual accounting mechanics that differ in the industry, not just repeat a generic list of services.
3. Identify which records the client supplies, which schedules Accounstone may prepare, and which decisions/approvals remain with the client or their qualified adviser.
4. Use real software names only where relevant; state that workflow depends on client access, exports and engagement scope.
5. Link to the relevant regional service pages and genuinely useful existing guides.
6. Have a unique title, meta description, canonical, one clear H1, appropriate Service/FAQ/Breadcrumb schema and a useful mobile layout.
7. Be registered in `docs/SEARCH-INTENTS.md`, `docs/ROUTES.md`, `lib/data.ts`, the content/industry depth records and `public/llms.txt`; sitemap generation must include it without duplicate URLs.
8. Be preview-tested for build state, rendered metadata, internal links and sitemap parity before it is considered complete.

## SEO and AEO are separate

**SEO checks:** primary intent and canonical owner, title/description, crawlable route, internal links, sitemap inclusion, competitor SERP/page comparison, duplicate/cannibalization risk, and later Search Console data if explicitly authorized.

**AEO checks:** a concise page-level answer to what the service is and who it is for; direct, evidence-aligned FAQ answers; named workflow inputs/outputs; scope boundaries; consistent company facts; and useful schema. Do not treat schema, `llms.txt` or FAQ markup as a ranking guarantee.

## Measurement and review

- Record the exact query and search date for competitor observations.
- Distinguish a page that appears in a search result from a confirmed ranking position.
- After the user authorizes GSC work, use impressions, clicks, query-to-page mapping and indexing status to revise priorities. Until then, do not claim performance or indexing outcomes.
- Review newly published pages after enough data accrues; consolidate or improve pages with overlapping intent rather than continually adding URLs.
