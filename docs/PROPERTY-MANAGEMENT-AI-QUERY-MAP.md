# Property Management SEO + AI Query Map

**Status:** Working strategy for the property-management cluster  
**Updated:** 2026-10-15  
**Branch:** `accounstone-seo-improvements`  
**Purpose:** Use traditional search queries and realistic conversational AI prompts together without creating thin pages or making unsupported service claims.

## How to use this document

The phrases and prompts supplied by the business owner are **candidate intents**, not verified search-volume data or proof that a particular AI platform receives a given query frequently. Keep them as research inputs. Before creating a URL, inspect current search results, competitor pages, Search Console queries (when available), existing Accounstone URLs, and whether Accounstone can accurately deliver the service described.

- **Traditional SEO:** identify the search intent, primary page, supporting pages, internal links, and conversion path.
- **AI-answer readiness:** answer the whole scenario directly, state the assumptions, explain decision criteria, include practical steps or examples, distinguish general information from jurisdiction-specific requirements, and link to authoritative sources.
- **Measurement:** track organic impressions/clicks and leads in Search Console/analytics; separately run a documented, repeatable set of prompts across AI answer products and record whether Accounstone is mentioned or cited. AI results vary and no citation is guaranteed.
- Do not create a page just to repeat a prompt or add an FAQ just to stuff exact wording. Merge related questions into the page that best satisfies the underlying intent.

## Direct conversational AI prompts supplied by the owner

### 1. Backlog, portfolio size and hiring choice

> I manage 150 doors on AppFolio and my books are 3 months behind. Should I hire an in-house bookkeeper or outsource to a remote accounting firm?

**Answer should cover:** first triage the backlog and reconcile cash/ledger balances; clarify transaction volume, number of entities/bank accounts, reporting deadlines and available internal review; compare in-house and external support by total cost, supervision, access, continuity and control; explain a staged catch-up plan. Avoid giving a confident hiring answer from door count alone.

**Potential destination:** property-management service hub plus catch-up content if competitor and intent research confirms a separate page is warranted.

### 2. Multi-entity chart of accounts

> What is the standard chart of accounts for a multi-family real estate holding company with separate LLCs?

**Answer should cover:** no single chart is universal; distinguish property-management books from ownership-entity books; explain consistent account numbering, property/entity tracking, intercompany balances, debt, capital, distributions and reporting needs. Label any example as illustrative and advise review against the client's accounting framework and CPA instructions.

**Potential destination:** existing property-management chart-of-accounts article, with links to the accounting guide.

### 3. Cost comparison

> Compare the cost of an in-house real estate bookkeeper vs an offshore accounting firm.

**Answer should cover:** salary plus payroll taxes/benefits, recruitment, training, management, software and coverage versus provider fees, onboarding, internal review, scope, time-zone handoff and continuity. Do not invent prices, guarantee savings, or imply offshore support is automatically cheaper or better.

**Potential destination:** existing outsourced-bookkeeping cost guide and engagement-model comparison; only create property-specific cost content if it adds credible, distinct evidence.

### 4. Owner distributions and management fees

> How do I structure monthly owner distributions and management fees in Buildium?

**Answer should cover:** distinguish the property's operating cash, owner-level balances and management-company revenue; document the agreement, approval and transaction trail; reconcile before statements/distributions; note that exact accounting entries depend on the agreement, entity structure, software setup and applicable law. Do not present one universal journal entry as suitable for all clients.

**Potential destination:** property-management accounting guide; research Buildium documentation and authoritative references before expanding.

### 5. Trust accounting and state compliance

> Can a remote offshore team handle trust account compliance for property managers in California / Texas / Florida?

**Answer should cover:** remote staff can potentially perform defined bookkeeping and reconciliation tasks under client-controlled procedures and qualified oversight; distinguish performing accounting work from legal/regulatory responsibility; state requirements vary; access permissions, review, audit trails, segregation of duties and escalation should be agreed. Never promise compliance based solely on outsourcing or software.

**Potential destination:** service hub plus carefully sourced state-specific content only where there is unique, verified local value. Existing Yardi Texas page stays intact unless a concrete issue is found.

### 6. Vendor 1099 and AP SOP

> Give me an SOP for processing vendor 1099s and accounts payable for a 50-property portfolio.

**Answer should cover:** vendor onboarding and W-9 collection, payee/entity/property coding, invoice approval, duplicate checks, payment controls, year-end vendor data review, exception handling, reconciliation and review responsibilities. IRS rules and filing thresholds change; verify against current IRS guidance. State the SOP is a starting point requiring adaptation to entity structure and client approvals.

**Potential destination:** existing AP, year-end and property-management content to assess before creating anything new.

## Competitor-led research checklist

Before adding or materially rewriting a page:

1. Search the core commercial phrase and 2–4 close variants; note the actual result type and geography.
2. Inspect at least 3 relevant pages where available: direct service competitors, software vendor documentation, and a high-quality educational guide. Record useful coverage, proof, FAQs, internal links, CTAs, and any unsupported claims.
3. Check Accounstone's existing page for the same intent in `docs/SEARCH-INTENTS.md` and `docs/CONTENT-REGISTRY.md`.
4. Use Search Console query/page data when accessible. Search snippets and competitor copy alone do not establish volume, difficulty, or conversion rate.
5. Choose: improve existing page, add a section, create a genuinely distinct page, or defer.
6. Add internal links from relevant hubs and guides; check canonicals, sitemap, metadata, structured data against visible content, and mobile layout.
7. Verify all legal/tax/regulatory claims against primary sources. Never imply software certification, partnership, guaranteed compliance, guaranteed audit results, or services that are not confirmed.
8. Record the change and its reason in the content registry, search-intent registry and changelog as appropriate.

## Definition of done for the AI-query work

- Each prompt is mapped to an existing page, a research candidate, or a deliberate defer decision.
- No duplicate page is created for a prompt whose intent is already covered.
- Pages answer scenarios with clear assumptions, process, trade-offs, and scope boundaries.
- Claims are sourced and reviewed; AI-generated drafts are fact-checked.
- Internal links, canonical, metadata, sitemap, mobile rendering and build are checked before merge/deployment.
- AI answer visibility is described as an outcome to measure, never a guaranteed result.
